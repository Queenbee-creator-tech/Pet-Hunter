# Creature system (working)

## Species rarity
- Common: white, 6 per biome
- Rare: blue, 12 per biome
- Epic: purple, 4 per biome
- Legendary: gold, 2 per biome

Rarity describes a species tier; mutations are a separate system. Normal mutations and exceptionally rare mutations are planned, but rates and effects are TBD.

## Growth
Baby → Adolescent → Young adult → Adult. Egg incubation, if relevant, precedes baby stage. Growth timing and care needs are TBD.

## Acquisition
Species-dependent possibilities: egg gathering, acquiring a juvenile, trust/taming, adult capture. Ecology, aggression, habitat and player choices should affect the story. Exact mechanics TBD.

## Combat and stats
Candidate stats: health, attack, defense, speed, stamina. Abilities may vary by rarity; legendary abilities and ability-less commons are possibilities, not finalized rules. Balancing and PvP come after initial worldbuilding.
