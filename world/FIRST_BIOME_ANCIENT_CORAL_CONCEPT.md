# First biome concept — Ancient Coral (working name)

**Status:** Working worldbuilding direction; names, lore, creatures and mechanics are not yet canon.

## Core environment
A mostly shallow, crystal-clear ocean with scattered exposed, **walkable land covered in towering coral forests**. Players can explore reef islands, coral paths, limestone platforms, caves and exposed formations on foot. The dominant terrain between these places is water, not continuous land. This is not a fully underwater setting: it should feel like an ancient underwater world has risen into open air.

## Travel fantasy
Crossing between coral forests and reef islands often requires **giant turtles or other rideable aquatic creatures**. Possible wooden homes, shelters or platforms on turtle backs. Exact transport rules, availability, settlement culture and creature species TBD.

## Visual reference cues
- Turquoise shallows, bright coastal light, massive reef towers and living coral trees
- Organic porous coral arches and cathedral-like formations
- Coral-covered walkable surfaces above the waterline
- Small reef islands and shallow channels interspersed with deeper routes
- Wood-and-rope structures integrated into a marine landscape
- User-provided reference images are inspiration, not assets licensed for inclusion

## Potential gameplay consequences (ideas only)
- Wading in shallow areas; aquatic mounts or transport needed for wider channels
- Tracking, climbing, and searching coral cavities for nests or young
- Access to encounters changes with equipment, skills, and transport
- Species-specific egg/baby/taming scenarios determined by the engine, narrated by LLM
