# Legendary breeding — design discussion (NOT CANON)

## Confirmed intent from discussion
- Common + common must not directly produce a legendary.
- Players should be able to obtain legendaries through difficult wild hunting/acquisition **and** advanced breeding/hatching.
- A potential special path: compatible epic parents may produce a legendary only if the player has adequate skill and follows exact, challenging breeding and egg-incubation conditions. Ordinary breeding produces an ordinary epic outcome.
- Avoid repeatable, effortless legendary production that undermines hunting or common creatures.
- Existing rarity palette: common white, rare blue, epic purple, legendary gold. Rarity vs mutation terminology still needs clarification with co-designer.

## Open proposals, not decisions
1. Legendary creatures are infertile.
2. Legendary parents produce lower-tier offspring.
3. Legendary reproduction is possible but gated by strict conditions, limited opportunities, special bloodlines, or other safeguards.
4. Wild legendary acquisition might introduce unique breeding potential; bred legendaries might have limits.

## Questions for balancing
- Can legendary + legendary reproduce at all? If yes, what can result?
- Should breeding require a scarce consumable, a skill challenge, a discovery, a cooldown, or a per-creature limit?
- How do commons remain relevant to progression and combat?
- How does the player learn special recipes without turning them into automatic infinite production?
- Are tier transformations new species, forms, or individual quality upgrades?

Do not implement until discussed and approved.


## Latest direction — user favors combining B + C
- Even legendary + legendary with correct conditions should **usually produce a lower-tier offspring**, potentially rare; legendary offspring should be very unlikely.
- Correct skill and incubation are necessary but do not guarantee the top-tier result.
- Explicit concern: players discovering the recipe and mass-producing legendaries would destroy rarity and hunting incentives.
- Design requirement: use structural anti-farming safeguards, not probability alone.
- Candidate safeguards to evaluate: limited attempts per legendary, restrictions on legendary-descended bloodlines, and server-authoritative lineage tracking. **These safeguards and exact odds are not yet approved.**
- Illustrative odds discussed: 80% rare / 19% epic / 1% legendary; strictly an example, not balanced or canonical.
- Test exploit paths including multi-account trading, unlimited rare breeding pairs, rerolls, save manipulation, and breeding descendants.
