# Pet Hunter — Naming Brainstorm (Paused)

Status: **Brainstorm only. No final game title or world name approved.**

## Current candidates
- **Pet Hunter** — temporary development title.
- **Ami** (singular), **Amis** (plural) — user's favorite candidate for collective term for collectible creatures. Individual species retain their own names. Inspired by animals and friends; not formally locked.
- **Avalora** — liked as a possible Autumn starting city name; not locked.
- **World name** — undecided.
- **Game title** — undecided, may differ from world name.

## Naming direction
The game is a mobile-first fantasy creature collecting, hatching, breeding, raising and exploration adventure across four exotic seasonal biomes. User wants a **catchy, short, cute but not childish, memorable, easy-to-say mobile game title** that sounds natural in 'Let's download/play ___'. Avoid repeatedly forcing 'wild' or formulaic fantasy suffixes. Kingdom-of titles felt too much like a setting, not an app/game name. Biology/animal kingdom/scientific wordplay is a promising inspiration, but no proposal has clicked yet.
