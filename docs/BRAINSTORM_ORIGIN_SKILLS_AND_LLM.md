# Brainstorming notes — origin skills, exotic biomes, and LLM encounters

**STATUS: NON-CANON / IDEAS ONLY.** Nothing in this document is an approved game rule.

## Exotic biomes
Take inspiration from the discovery and creature husbandry of ARK and the text interaction of Hatchery, while developing original exotic environments. Forest, ice, desert, and volcanic are starting categories, not necessarily conventional landscapes. Subregions, names, and ecosystems remain open.

## Starting home biome and skills
At character creation, the player might choose a biome to live in or originate from. That choice could grant starting familiarity and skills that help track, hunt, capture, tame, or find eggs/young in that biome.

Examples to explore (not fixed):
- Forest: climbing, traps, tracking through foliage.
- Ice: snow tracking, cold survival, ice navigation.
- Desert: sand tracking, locating dens, resource conservation.
- Volcanic: hazardous terrain navigation, heat survival.

An unfamiliar biome should be more challenging, but cross-biome skills could be learned later. Avoid permanently locking players out until design is discussed.

## Meaningful difficulty
Finding eggs, capturing juveniles, and taming creatures should involve species-specific obstacles, preparation, player skills, risks, and choices. No universal one-click acquisition. Exact difficulty formulas and penalties TBD.

## LLM-driven text story mode
Proposed split:
1. Engine selects legal actions, encounters, creatures, odds, success/failure, and authoritative changes to player/creature state.
2. LLM narrates the authorized outcome and offers story flavor within approved lore, biome and species profiles.
3. Mobile-first text interface shows narrative and touch-friendly choices.

The LLM should not create new canonical species, override success/failure, award creatures or items, change stats, or contradict approved world data. Validate any model-generated choices and outputs before applying state changes. Consider reusable structured encounter templates and fallback narration if an LLM is unavailable.

## Open design questions
- Does home biome mean birthplace, current settlement, or both?
- Which skills exist, how are they gained, and can all be learned?
- How much does origin affect odds and encounters?
- Are there predefined story arcs alongside generated encounters?
- How are lore constraints and LLM costs handled on mobile?

These notes are for discussion, not implementation approval.
