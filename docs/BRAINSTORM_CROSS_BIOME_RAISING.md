# Cross-biome egg raising and habitat preparation — brainstorming

**STATUS: NON-CANON.** Record of ideas, not finalized rules.

## Player scenario
A player whose home is the warm Ancient Coral biome travels to an ice/winter biome, finds an egg, and returns home. The home climate and the character's starting skill set may be incompatible with the egg. Obtaining an egg does **not** automatically mean it can be hatched or raised.

## Candidate systems
- **Character expertise:** biome-specific skills for acquiring, incubating, feeding and handling creatures. Skills can potentially be learned beyond the starting biome.
- **Constructed microhabitats:** players build appropriate environments for imported eggs and growing creatures (e.g. a cold incubation chamber in a warm coastal home).
- **Guidebook knowledge:** species profiles document known incubation conditions, diet, growth and care; players may need to discover requirements.
- **Cross-biome progression:** travel, resource collection, research and building may be required before a nonnative creature can be raised.
- **Engine authority:** engine checks skills, knowledge, environmental conditions and resources. The LLM narrates the valid outcome; it does not waive requirements.

## Important open design decisions
- Can players safely store an incompatible egg until they can prepare a habitat? For how long?
- Are knowledge and habitat both mandatory, or can special equipment substitute for some skill?
- Is there a risk of failed incubation, or is the action simply unavailable until requirements are met?
- Does a hatchling need a different environment than its egg or adult form?
- How complex should habitat building be in the initial mobile text interface?

Avoid hard-locking players out of other biomes unless explicitly decided. Do not implement until approved.
