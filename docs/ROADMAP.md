# Development roadmap (provisional)

1. **Universe foundation:** four biome concepts, species tiers, growth stages, creature template.
2. **First biome worldbuilding:** name, environment, habitats, full candidate roster, acquisition story concepts.
3. **Core systems design:** species stats, rarity balance, mutations, growth and species-specific acquisition rules.
4. **Engine prototype:** implement data-driven encounters, capture/raising, and persistence independent of UI.
5. **Mobile text prototype:** portrait-first responsive interface with touch choices and save/resume.
6. **Visual expansion:** creature and biome graphics; evaluate 2D/3D/mobile app pathways.
7. **Combat and PvP:** design, balance, test, and implement after the foundation is stable.

## Open decisions
- Which biome is developed first?
- Names, habitats and species concepts?
- Stats, abilities, mutation rules and encounter odds?
- Combat rules and multiplayer architecture?
- Browser-first only or eventually packaged mobile app?
